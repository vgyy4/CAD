# ADR 0062: Ribs

Status: accepted (2026-11-24)

## Context
SolidWorks (`moRib_c`), Solid Edge (`JMRibAssocFunction`) and Inventor ribs thicken open sketch
lines into a wall and grow it up to the part. Gusset had no such feature: six SolidWorks parts
whose only unsupported feature is a rib came out 5-22 % short (ADR 0043 amendments sw18,
sw19), and Solid Edge's smart-boss ribs were built as an `extrude ... to: next, op: new` from
the rib's foot (ADR 0061 amendment 2026-11-23). A rib's lines are usually drawn short of the
walls they join: the CAD systems extend them until they meet the part.

Written from OCCT's public API documentation and headers and from the files' own structure;
nothing from another CAD system's code.

## Decision
**Document** (`FeatureKind::Rib`, `RibDef`):

```
rib Rib1 (path: Sketch3[.curves(l1, l2)], thickness: 5 mm, toward: [0, -1] [, side: front|back])
rib Rib2 (path: Sketch4, thickness: 3 mm, normal: true [, reverse: true] [, side: left|right])
```

* `path`: one chain of straight lines of a sketch (all its non-construction lines, or the
  named ones). Arcs and splines are refused ("a rib is drawn with straight lines").
* **In the plane** (`toward: [x, y]`, a direction in the sketch's axes): each line's material
  grows in the sketch plane, square to the line, on the side `toward` points to; the thickness
  lies across the plane, centred (default) or all to the `front` (along the sketch normal) or
  `back`.
* **Along the normal** (`normal: true`, `reverse` against it): the chain thickened in the plane
  (mitred at its joints) grows along the sketch normal; the thickness is centred, or all to the
  `left`/`right` of the chain seen from the front, the chain running from its first line.
* The chain's free ends are extended past the body's box, so the rib reaches the walls beside
  it as the CAD systems' ribs do ("linear" extension). It is joined to the target body
  (`body:`, default the most recent).
* Validation: the sketch must exist and be a sketch, curves exist and are not listed twice,
  `toward` and `normal` exclude each other, `reverse` needs `normal`, `side` must fit the
  growth (front/back in the plane, left/right along the normal).

**Kernel** (`Kernel::rib_to_next(profile, target, reverse)`): ADR 0061's up-to-next sweep
(flag 4 of `gs_extrude_to_next`, remote request 41, protocol 14): a prism longer than the
target's box is split by the target; the start pieces outside the material that stop on it are
the rib. Pieces that reach the prism's far end (from the extended ends outside the part) are
dropped instead of refused; no piece stopping on the target is an error ("the rib meets no
face of the body beside its lines").

**Evaluation** (`gusset-eval/src/rib.rs`): in the plane, one rectangle per line in the plane
through the line square to the sketch (the line, extended at free ends, by the thickness),
swept square to the line; along the normal, the chain's mitred outline in the sketch plane.
Each piece is joined to the body in turn. Faces are named as a sheet-metal cross-section's
(ADR 0037): `Rib1/flat(l1)`, `flat_back(l1)`, `start(l1)`, `end(l1)`, `cap(start)`.

**Command** `feature.rib` (sketch, curves, thickness, toward or normal/reverse, side, body);
GUI = CLI = MCP (ADR 0012). **Tree import** (`type: "rib"`, version 2): `sketch`, `curves`,
`thickness` (mm), `toward` (model-space direction in the sketch plane) or `normal` (model-space
direction along the sketch normal), `side` (model-space direction the thickness goes to, for a
one-sided rib in the plane), `target`.

## Consequences
* Golden tests (OCCT): a gusset in an L bracket from a line drawn short of both walls (exact
  triangle volume), one-sided thickness, a rib along the normal down into a tray, and the
  refusal when the rib grows away from the part.
* Not supported: arcs and splines in a rib, draft on a rib's sides (Inventor's taper,
  SolidWorks' draft), "natural" extension along curves, ribs in the plane whose lines meet at
  joints are joined line by line (each line's rectangle swept square to it), so an outer corner
  of a bent in-plane rib is not filled.
